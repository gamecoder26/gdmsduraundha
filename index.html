About our school

A place to learn, discover and grow

G.D. Memorial School welcomes families from Duraudha and nearby communities. Our school is dedicated to supporting students as they learn, develop confidence and prepare for the opportunities ahead.

We believe education is about more than lessons — it is also about curiosity, responsibility, respect and the confidence to keep learning.

📘 LearningBuild knowledge step by step.
🌱 GrowthEncourage curiosity and confidence.
🤝 RespectValue kindness and responsibility.
🏫 CommunityWork together with families.
Our leadership

Built on a founder’s vision

G.D. Memorial School honours the vision and contribution of its founder, late Shri Shiojee Chaudhary, a teacher whose name remains at the heart of the institution.

“Education helps young people build knowledge, character and the confidence to shape their future.”
Late Shri Shiojee Chaudhary Founder · Teacher
